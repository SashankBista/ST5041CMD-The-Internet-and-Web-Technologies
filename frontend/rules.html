the rules.

Read these before you play. Joining an event means you agree to all of them.

Authorized targets Teams and accounts Flags and submissions Scoring Fair play Penalties Privacy and logging

Authorized targets only

The most important rule

Attack only the challenge machines and applications listed on the challenge board. Everything else is off limits, including this platform, other players and any network you were not given.

  • In scopeChallenge servers, files and services published by the organizers during the event window.
  • Out of scopeThe Rootline platform itself, the event network, other teams and any third-party system.
  • Not sure?Ask an organizer first. If it is not listed, treat it as out of scope.

Teams and accounts

  • One account eachEvery player registers one account. Duplicate or fake accounts are removed.
  • Team sizeA team has between one and four members. Players can belong to one team only.
  • PasswordsUse a strong, unique password and never share it. You are responsible for your account.
  • Team namesNo offensive, misleading or impersonating names. Organizers may rename a team.

Flags and submissions

  • FormatFlags look like FLAG{...}. Submit the whole string including the braces.
  • Attempt limitEach team can submit 10 flags per minute. Going over locks submissions for 60 seconds.
  • SharingNever share flags or solutions with other teams during the event, publicly or privately.
  • Broken challengeIf a challenge seems broken, tell an organizer. Do not exploit the fault.

Scoring

Points go down as more teams solve a challenge, so earlier solves are worth more. A challenge never drops below its minimum value.

  • First solveThe full base value of the challenge.
  • Later solvesThe value falls with each solve down to the minimum.
  • HintsSome hints cost points. The cost is shown before you unlock one.
  • TiesEqual scores are ranked by whichever team reached the score first.

Fair play

  • No brute forceDo not script flag guessing or hammer the login. The rate limit exists for this.
  • No denial of serviceDo not flood, crash or overload challenge servers or the platform.
  • No collusionTeams compete separately. Working together across teams is not allowed.
  • No outside helpUsing tools and public references is fine. Asking someone else to solve it for you is not.

Penalties

Penalties depend on how serious the breach is and are decided by the organizers.

MinorWarning and removal of the affected points.
SeriousPoints removed and a temporary suspension.
SevereDisqualification and a ban from future events.

Privacy and logging

To keep the event fair and secure, Rootline records logins, submissions and administrative actions. Logs are used only to run the event, investigate rule breaks and report results. Passwords are stored as hashes and are never visible to organizers.
